**Elbify is here.** Elbify is a free Discord bot and website for running Emergency Hamburg private roleplay servers. It launches with a dashboard to set up your server, an Admin Panel for your staff team, department portals, custom forms, session announcements, shift tracking and Roblox verification, all available in ten languages. This page lists everything Elbify launches with. For the highlights, see [What's New](/pages/bulletin/whats-new).

<Update label="Release" description="Elbify launches" tags={["Website","Dashboard","Admin Panel","Bot"]}>
  ## Website and Account

  **Sign in with Discord** <br />Sign in on the Elbify website with your Discord account to manage your servers, fill in forms and join departments.

  **Three themes** <br />Choose **Light**, **Elbify** or **Dark** in **Settings** under **Appearance**. Dark is the default.

  **Ten languages** <br />Elbify is available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian and Turkish. Pick yours from the language menu in the navigation bar. Spotted a mistake? Use the **Report a translation error** form.

  **Accessibility settings** <br />In **Settings** under **Accessibility**, turn on **Reduce Motion**, **High Contrast**, **Larger Text**, **Underline Links** or a **Dyslexia-Friendly Font**. Changes apply instantly.

  **Account settings** <br />Set a **preferred name** to show in the navigation bar, and see your linked Discord and Roblox accounts in one place.

  **Search** <br />Search the website and the documentation from the navigation bar. Press `/` to open it from anywhere.

  **Notifications** <br />Platform news and updates, such as a finished data import, arrive in the **Notifications** section of your profile menu. Mark them read one by one or all at once.

  **Status page** <br />The **Status** page shows whether Elbify's systems are up, with uptime over the last 30 days.

  **Cookie preferences** <br />Choose **Accept all**, **Necessary only** or **Manage preferences**. Your theme, language and accessibility settings are only remembered between visits if you allow preference cookies; otherwise they last until you close the tab. Change your choice at any time from **Cookie preferences** in the footer.

  ## Dashboard

  **Server picker** <br />The **Dashboard** lists every server you can manage, with buttons to open its **Dashboard** or its **Admin Panel**.

  **Organised settings** <br />Server settings are grouped in the sidebar into **Core**, **Organisation** and **Plugin Settings**.

  **Plugins** <br />Turn on only the plugins your server needs. When a page needs a plugin that is off, it explains what to do, with a button that takes staff with **Manage Plugins** straight to the plugin. See the [Plugins](/pages/dashboard/plugins) page.

  **Commands** <br />Turn every command and subcommand on or off for your server. See the [Commands](/pages/dashboard/commands) page.

  **Permissions** <br />Choose a **Mod Role** and an **Admin Role**, and add **Permission Roles** to decide exactly what each staff role can do, starting from a preset. See the [Permissions](/pages/dashboard/permissions) page.

  **Audit Logs** <br />A categorised history of activity in your server, covering the dashboard, commands, moderations, reports, appeals and the Admin Panel. Dangerous dashboard actions are flagged. See the [Audit Logs](/pages/dashboard/audit-logs) page.

  **Unsaved changes** <br />Settings pages show a save bar while you have changes, and warn you before you leave a page without saving.

  **Discord audit log reasons** <br />Every change Elbify makes in your Discord server, from the website or the bot, includes a reason in your server's Discord audit log.

  ## Admin Panel

  The Admin Panel is where your staff team works during a session. See the [Admin Panel](/pages/bulletin/admin-panel) page for the full guide.

  **Live stats** <br />Open callouts, staff on shift, active tasks and logged moderations at the top of the panel.

  **Your shift** <br />Start and end your shift and take breaks from the website, with a live timer.

  **Weekly leaderboard** <br />See everyone's hours this week against the weekly requirement, with staff on leave marked **On Leave**.

  **My task** <br />See the task you are assigned and resolve it from the panel.

  **Callouts** <br />Resolve or dismiss admin callouts, with a message to the caller and a staff-only note.

  **Moderation log** <br />Log moderations with a Roblox player search, then browse, filter, edit and delete them. Staff can edit and delete their own moderations; staff with **Manage Moderations** can edit and delete any. Edits and deletions are posted to your moderation log channel.

  **Tracking** <br />The 50 most recent actions taken on the panel.

  **Setup guide** <br />Servers without staff roles are walked through setting up the panel on the **Permissions** page.

  ## Departments

  **Department portals** <br />Create departments for your server, each with its own member portal. Choose a type when you create one: **Police**, **Firefighter**, **Medic**, **HARS**, **Taxi / Bus Company**, **Trucker** or **Government**. See the [Departments](/pages/dashboard/departments) page.

  **Setup wizard** <br />Set up a department in three steps, with an optional icon, Department Role and log channel.

  **Join links** <br />Members join a department through its private join link.

  **Shouts** <br />Post announcements on the department's home page, tag the ranks they're meant for, and schedule shouts for later. Scheduled shouts are listed under **Upcoming** until they post.

  **Roster and ranks** <br />Drag members between ranks on the roster to update the chain of command. Create ranks, control what each one can do, and drag them to reorder the hierarchy. Link ranks to a Discord role or Roblox group rank, and members are given the matching rank automatically.

  **Divisions and positions** <br />Government departments come with divisions, each with its own ranks and role, and government positions, shown as the **Cabinet**.

  **Discord roles and logs** <br />Members are given the Department Role when they join and Division Roles when they move into a division. Roster, rank and shout activity is posted to the department's log channels.

  | Limit | Departments | Government departments |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Departments per server | 3 | 3 |
  | Ranks per department | 15 | 30 |
  | Divisions per department | 0 | 4 |
  | Ranks per division | 0 | 15 |
  | Positions per department | 0 | 6 |

  ## Forms

  **Reports and appeals** <br />Players report rule breaks and appeal moderations on the Elbify website. Choose which moderation types can be appealed; leaving the list empty allows every type. See the [Player Reports](/pages/plugins/player-reports) and [Appeals](/pages/plugins/appeals) pages.

  **Custom forms** <br />Create your own forms, such as staff applications, with up to 20 questions each. Questions can be a short answer, a long answer or a selection. A server can have **1** custom form. The report and appeal forms don't count towards this. See the [Forms](/pages/dashboard/forms) page.

  **Review on the dashboard or in Discord** <br />Reviewers accept or deny submissions on the dashboard, or with the buttons on the submission message in Discord.

  **Roles on decision** <br />Give people a role when their submission is accepted, and another when it is denied. If Elbify can't give the role, the reviewer is told privately.

  **Attachments** <br />People can attach images to their answers.

  **Server forms page** <br />Each server has a page listing its live forms. Only members of the server can see and fill in its forms, except the appeal form, which anyone can use.

  ## Sessions

  **Session announcements** <br />Announce a session start, low player count and shutdown with `/session ssu`, `/session lpc` and `/session ssd`. Design each announcement on the dashboard with a live preview of how it looks in Discord. See the [Session Management](/pages/plugins/session-management) page.

  **Join details** <br />Add your server's join code (up to 8 characters) and join link once, and they are used in all three announcements, with a **Join Session** button.

  **Pings** <br />Choose a role to ping for each announcement, and optionally mention the mod role too.

  **Session transfer** <br />Hand the current session to another host, or add and remove co-hosts, with `/session transfer`. The announcement is updated to show who is running the session.

  **Session polls** <br />Ask members whether they would join a session with `/session poll`, open for 1 minute to 24 hours. Members press **Vote**, staff can press **Voters**, and the **Session Poll Votes** card on the dashboard shows the votes live.

  **Session logs** <br />Log every session start, low player count, transfer and shutdown, and every session poll with its final vote count, to the channels you choose. The member who posted a poll can get a DM with the final count.

  ## Moderation and Admin Callouts

  **Moderation logs** <br />Log moderations with `/moderate` or on the Admin Panel, using moderation types you set for your server, with an optional staff-only note. See the [Moderation](/pages/plugins/moderation) page.

  **Moderations in Discord** <br />List moderations with `/moderations`, view a case with `/moderation-info`, and edit or delete one with `/edit-moderation`.

  **Admin callouts** <br />Players call for staff with `/call-admin`, giving a reason and location. Staff see open callouts with `/admincall list` and on the Admin Panel. See the [Admin Callouts](/pages/plugins/admin-callouts) page.

  **Import Data** <br />Bring your moderation history over from Trident. Elbify reads the moderation logs Trident posted in a channel and adds them to your moderation history in the background. Map imported types to your own, and cancel or delete an import at any time. A server can run **3** imports. See the [Import Data](/pages/dashboard/import-data) page.

  ## Shifts, Tasks and Leave

  **Shifts** <br />Staff clock in and out with `/shift` or on the Admin Panel. Set the required weekly hours, the longest break allowed and shift types with their own roles and log channels. Review progress with `/shiftadmin`. See the [Shifts](/pages/plugins/shifts) page.

  **Tasks** <br />Give on-shift staff duties at an interval you choose. Staff resolve a task with the **Resolve Task** button, `/task resolve` or the Admin Panel, and get the next one straight away. Tasks pause while staff are on break. See the [Tasks](/pages/plugins/tasks) page.

  **Leave of absence** <br />Staff request leave with `/leave request` or in **Settings** under **Leave of Absence**. Management accepts it, accepts it with different dates, or denies it, in Discord or on the dashboard. Staff on accepted leave can be excused from shift checks. See the [Leave of Absence](/pages/plugins/leave-of-absence) page.

  ## Verification and Vehicles

  **Roblox verification** <br />Members link their Roblox account to get a role and a synced nickname, with the verification panel, `/verify`, or the verify page on the website. Staff use `/update` and `/verification` to manage it. See the [Verification](/pages/plugins/verification) page.

  **Manage verification across servers** <br />See every server you are verified in, spot servers verified with a different Roblox account, and update one or all of them at once.

  **Vehicle registration** <br />Players register their vehicles with `/vehicle register`, and staff look up any vehicle by its plate with `/vehicle search`. See the [Vehicle Registration](/pages/plugins/vehicle-registration) page.

  ## Help and Contact

  **`/help`** <br />Links to the Elbify website, our support server, and your server's **Dashboard** and **Admin Panel**.

  **`/language`** <br />Set the language Elbify uses in your server.

  **Contact the Elbify team** <br />Three forms on the website go straight to the Elbify team: **Report an Elbify team member**, **Report a translation error** and **Report a missing vehicle**.

  ## Privacy and Security

  - Evidence you upload is stored on Elbify's own file storage.
  - Elbify keeps server-side security logs, such as IP addresses, to protect the website from abuse. These are kept whatever cookie choice you make and are deleted after 90 days.
