This is the full update log for the new **Elbify website**, and the **Elbify bot** update that ships with it. The website has been rebuilt from the ground up, with a redesigned dashboard, a new Admin Panel for staff, department portals, custom forms, and support for ten languages. The bot gains session transfers and polls, moderation management in Discord, reworked verification and resolvable tasks. For a short overview, see [What's New](/pages/bulletin/whats-new).

<Update label="Release" tags={["Website","Dashboard","Admin Panel","Bot"]} description="The New Elbify Website">

## Website and Account

**Redesigned website** <br />A new home page with an animated tour of Elbify's features, a new navigation bar, and a mobile menu built for phones and tablets.

**Three themes** <br />Choose **Light**, **Elbify** or **Dark** in **Settings** under **Appearance**. Dark is the default.

**Ten languages** <br />The website is available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian and Turkish. Pick yours from the language menu in the navigation bar. Spotted a mistake? Use the **Report a translation error** form.

**Accessibility settings** <br />In **Settings** under **Accessibility**, turn on **Reduce Motion**, **High Contrast**, **Larger Text**, **Underline Links** or a **Dyslexia-Friendly Font**. Changes apply instantly.

**Account settings** <br />Set a **preferred name** to show in the navigation bar, and see your linked Discord, Roblox and Patreon accounts in one place.

**Search** <br />Search the website and the documentation from the navigation bar. Press `/` to open it from anywhere.

**Notifications** <br />Platform news and updates, such as a finished data import, now arrive in the **Notifications** section of your profile menu. Mark them read one by one or all at once.

**Status page** <br />The new **Status** page shows whether Elbify's systems are up, with uptime over the last 30 days.

**Cookie preferences** <br />A cookie banner lets you choose **Accept all**, **Necessary only** or **Manage preferences**. Your theme, language and accessibility settings are only remembered between visits if you allow preference cookies; otherwise they last until you close the tab. You can change your choice at any time from **Cookie preferences** in the footer.

**Updated legal pages** <br />The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy have been updated to cover the new website.

## Dashboard

**Server picker** <br />The **Dashboard** lists every server you can manage, with buttons to open its **Dashboard** or its **Admin Panel**.

**New layout** <br />Server settings are grouped in the sidebar into **Core**, **Organisation** and **Plugin Settings**.

**Plugin setup guides** <br />When a page needs a plugin that is turned off, it now explains what to do, with a button that takes staff with **Manage Plugins** straight to the plugin.

**Unsaved changes** <br />Settings pages show a save bar while you have changes, and warn you before you leave a page without saving.

**Permissions** <br />Choose a **Mod Role** and an **Admin Role**, and add **Permission Roles** to decide exactly what each staff role can do, starting from a preset. See the [Permissions](/pages/dashboard/permissions) page.

**Audit Logs** <br />A categorised history of activity in your server, covering the dashboard, commands, moderations, reports, appeals and the Admin Panel. Dangerous dashboard actions are flagged. See the [Audit Logs](/pages/dashboard/audit-logs) page.

**Discord audit log reasons** <br />Changes the website makes in your Discord server now include a reason in your server's Discord audit log.

## Admin Panel

The Admin Panel has been redesigned. See the [Admin Panel](/pages/bulletin/admin-panel) page for the full guide.

**Live stats** <br />Open callouts, staff on shift, active tasks and logged moderations at the top of the panel.

**Your shift** <br />Start and end your shift and take breaks from the website, with a live timer.

**Weekly leaderboard** <br />See everyone's hours this week against the weekly requirement, with staff on leave marked **On Leave**.

**My task** <br />See the task you are assigned and resolve it from the panel. Tasks pause while you are on break and are released when your shift ends.

**Callouts** <br />Resolve or dismiss admin callouts, with a message to the caller and a staff-only note.

**Moderation log** <br />Log moderations with a Roblox player search, then browse, filter, edit and delete them. Staff can edit and delete their own moderations; staff with **Manage Moderations** can edit and delete any. Edits and deletions are posted to your moderation log channel.

**Tracking** <br />The 50 most recent actions taken on the panel.

**Setup guide** <br />Servers without staff roles are walked through setting up the panel on the **Permissions** page.

## Departments

**Department portals** <br />Create departments for your server and give each its own portal. Choose a type when you create one: **Police**, **Firefighter**, **Medic**, **HARS**, **Taxi & Bus**, **Trucker** or **Government**.

**Setup wizard** <br />Set up a new department in three steps.

**Department icons** <br />Upload a square PNG, JPG, WEBP or GIF up to 2 MB. It is shown in the portal, on the dashboard and on join links. Without one, the department type's icon is used.

**Join links** <br />Members join a department through its join link.

**Shouts** <br />Post announcements on the department's home page, ping chosen ranks, and schedule shouts for later.

**Roster and ranks** <br />Drag members between ranks on the roster to update the chain of command. Create ranks, control what each one can do, and drag them to reorder the hierarchy. **Sync Ranks** updates members' ranks in one go.

**Divisions and positions** <br />Divisions have their own rank hierarchy and permissions. Positions are unique offices that one member holds at a time.

**Government departments** <br />Come with divisions, government positions shown as the **Cabinet**, and log channels for each category.

| Limit | Departments | Government departments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Departments per server | 3 | 3 |
| Ranks per department | 15 | 30 |
| Divisions per department | 0 | 4 |
| Ranks per division | 0 | 15 |
| Positions per department | 0 | 6 |

## Forms

**Custom forms** <br />Create your own forms, such as staff applications, with up to 20 questions each. Questions can be a short answer, a long answer or a selection. A server can have **1** custom form, or **5** with Elbify Plus. The built-in report and appeal forms don't count towards this. See the [Forms](/pages/dashboard/forms) page.

**Reviewer roles** <br />Members with reviewer roles can accept or deny submissions on the dashboard and with buttons in Discord.

**Roles on decision** <br />Give people a role when their submission is accepted, and another when it is denied. **1** role for each, or **5** with Elbify Plus.

**Attachments** <br />People can attach images to their answers.

**Server forms page** <br />Each server has a page listing its live forms. Only members of the server can see and fill in its forms, except the appeal form, which anyone can use.

**Redesigned report and appeal forms** <br />The public report and appeal pages have been redesigned. Players can only appeal moderation types you allow, and leaving the list empty allows every type.

**Your own report and appeal questions** <br />With Elbify Plus, customise the questions on the built-in report and appeal forms with the question builder: add, edit, reorder, duplicate and delete questions.

**New date and file pickers** <br />Date and time fields and file uploads have new pickers across the website.

## Session Management

**Redesigned page** <br />Edit the **Session Start**, **Low Player Count** and **Session Shutdown** announcements in tabs, with a live preview of how each looks in Discord. See the [Session Management](/pages/plugins/session-management) page.

**Join details** <br />Add your server's join code (up to 8 characters) and join link once, and they are used in all three announcements.

**Pings** <br />Choose a role to ping for each announcement, and optionally mention the mod role too.

**Session polls** <br />Configure session polls posted with `/session poll`, with a duration from 1 minute to 24 hours. The **Session Poll Votes** card on the dashboard shows live votes on current polls and updates every 15 seconds.

**Session transfer** <br />The new session transfer and session poll commands can be turned on or off on the **Commands** page.

**Session logs** <br />Choose a channel to log every session start, low player count, transfer and shutdown, and a channel to log when session polls are posted and end, with the final vote count. Optionally DM the member who posted a poll the final count.

## Staff Tools

**Shift Settings** <br />Set the required weekly hours, the longest break allowed and the shift log channel, and manage shift types. See the [Shifts](/pages/plugins/shifts) page.

**Tasks** <br />Manage your task types, the channel tasks are announced in, and how often tasks are assigned to on-shift staff. See the [Tasks](/pages/plugins/tasks) page.

**Leave of absence** <br />Staff request leave from **Settings** under **Leave of Absence**, and management can accept them, accept them with different dates, or deny them on the dashboard. See the [Leave of Absence](/pages/plugins/leave-of-absence) page.

**Moderation settings** <br />Moderation and Admin Callouts settings now require the **Server Management** permission.

## Import Data

Bring your moderation history over from another bot. Elbify reads the moderation logs Trident posted in a channel and adds them to your moderation history. See the [Import Data](/pages/dashboard/import-data) page.

- Imports run in the background, so you can close the page. You get a notification when an import is done.
- Imported moderation types that don't match yours can be mapped to an existing type or added as a new one.
- Cancel an import, or delete everything an import brought in, at any time.
- A server can run **3** imports, or **10** with Elbify Plus. Plus servers import about 3× faster and can scan more messages.

## Verification and Vehicles

**Redesigned verify page** <br />The verify page shows the server you are verifying in, your verification status and the Roblox account you are verifying with, and lets you switch accounts. See the [Verification](/pages/plugins/verification) page.

**Manage verification across servers** <br />See every server you are verified in, spot servers verified with a different Roblox account, and update one or all of them at once.

**Roblox account in the profile menu** <br />Once you link a Roblox account, the profile menu links to it in **Settings**.

**Vehicle registration limit removed** <br />Servers no longer set a limit on how many vehicles each player can register. See the [Vehicle Registration](/pages/plugins/vehicle-registration) page.

**Missing vehicles** <br />Report a vehicle that's missing from Elbify with the **Report a missing vehicle** form.

## Elbify Plus

**Plus is per server** <br />Elbify Plus now belongs to a server, not an account. Anyone who manages the server gets its higher limits.

**New Plus page** <br />The rebuilt Plus page compares Free and Plus using the same limits the bot and dashboard check against.

**Nothing is deleted when Plus ends** <br />Your server goes back to the Free limits. Everything you made keeps working and can still be edited or removed; you just can't add more until you are under the Free limit.

| Limit | Free | Plus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task types | 5 | 25 |
| Shift types | 3 | 25 |
| Permission roles | 10 | 50 |
| Moderation types | 5 | 25 |
| Custom forms | 1 | 5 |
| Roles per form decision | 1 | 5 |
| Data imports | 3 | 10 |
| Your own questions on reports and appeals | No | Yes |

<Note>
  Need a single limit raised without Plus? Ask in our [Discord community](https://discord.gg/tTyTTW3Dta). The team can raise a limit for a server when it makes sense.
</Note>

## Discord Bot

### Sessions

**`/session transfer`** <br />Hand the current session to another host, add a co-host or remove one. The session start announcement is updated to show the new host and co-hosts. Only the current host or a server admin can use it.

**`/session poll`** <br />Post a poll asking members whether they would join a session. Members press **Vote**, and staff with **Session Management** can press **Voters** to see who voted. Set how long voting stays open with `duration:`, such as `30m` or `1h30m`.

**Session logs** <br />Session starts, low player counts, transfers and shutdowns, and session polls being posted and ending, are logged to the channels you choose on the dashboard. The member who posted a poll gets a DM with the final vote count when it ends, unless you turn this off.

See the [Session Management](/pages/plugins/session-management) page.

### Moderation

**`/moderations`** <br />List recent moderations, filtered by Roblox username or moderator.

**`/moderation-info`** <br />Show a moderation by its case number, including its staff note.

**`/edit-moderation`** <br />Change the type, reason or staff note of a moderation, or delete it. Staff can edit their own moderations; staff with **Manage Moderations** can edit any.

**Matching logs** <br />Moderation edits and deletions made in Discord are posted to your moderation log channel in the same format as edits made on the website.

**Staff notes** <br />`/moderate` has an optional `note:` for a staff-only note that's never shown to the player.

See the [Moderation](/pages/plugins/moderation) page.

### Tasks

**Resolve your task** <br />Press **Resolve Task** on the task announcement, or use `/task resolve`. If you are still on shift, you get your next task straight away.

**`/task view`** <br />See your current task and when it was assigned.

**Pause and release** <br />Tasks pause while you are on break, and are released when your shift ends or you leave the server.

See the [Tasks](/pages/plugins/tasks) page.

### Verification

**`/verify`** <br />Verify yourself, or another member with `member:` if you have permission.

**`/update`** <br />Refresh a member's verification roles and nickname.

**`/verification`** <br />`panel`, `remove` and `sync` now live under one command. `/verification panel` can also set a verification log channel.

**Rewritten verification panel** <br />The panel now explains what verifying does and how to do it, and works for servers that only use synced nicknames.

See the [Verification](/pages/plugins/verification) page.

### Forms

**Review in Discord** <br />Reviewers can accept or deny form submissions with the buttons on the submission message. If Elbify can't give a decision role, the reviewer is told privately, and the decision is still saved.

**Reports and appeals on the website** <br />The `/report` and `/appeal` commands have been removed. Players now submit reports and appeals on the Elbify website. See the [Player Reports](/pages/plugins/player-reports) and [Appeals](/pages/plugins/appeals) pages.

### General

**`/help`** <br />Now includes buttons to your server's **Dashboard** and **Admin Panel**.

**Command switches** <br />Every command and subcommand can be turned on or off on the dashboard's **Commands** page. See the [Commands](/pages/dashboard/commands) page.

**Ten languages** <br />The bot's replies are translated into the same ten languages as the website.

**Discord audit log reasons** <br />Every role, nickname, kick, thread, invite and message change the bot makes now includes a reason in your server's Discord audit log.

**More reliable commands** <br />Slow commands, buttons and menus no longer fail with an "Unknown interaction" error.

## Security

- Evidence you upload is now stored on Elbify's own file storage.
- Elbify keeps server-side security logs, such as IP addresses, to protect the website from abuse. These are kept whatever cookie choice you make and are deleted after 90 days.
