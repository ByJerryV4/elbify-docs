Welcome to the official **Elbify** documentation, the ultimate resource for learning more about Elbify, its powerful roleplay-enhancing features, and how to use them.

Elbify is a free-to-use, simple and powerful bot designed to enhance your Emergency Hamburg private roleplay experience. Our main goal is to provide for the communities which use Elbify on a daily basis, providing them with tools to help run roleplay servers without any hassle.
