  **The Report form must be live in the "Forms" core section on the dashboard in order for this plugin to work as intended.**
</Info>

## Reporting a Player

Reporting a player is really simple and can be done on the website **Forms** _(insert link here)._ Select the server you want to report the player in and complete the form.

**Servers may have their own form configurations.** Always check information with the server if you're unsure - Elbify Staff **cannot** assist you with server form inquiries.

---

Once the form is filled out and looks correct, submit the report. If the plugin is enabled, it'll be sent to a private staff channel of the server where the staff will thoroughly review your report.

You **cannot** edit or delete reports once they are submitted. Before submitting, ensure everything is correct.
