## In Discord

The Elbify bot works hand in hand with the website.

**Sessions** <br />Announce sessions with `/session`, hand them to another host or add co-hosts with `/session transfer`, and ask who would join with `/session poll`. See [Session Management](/pages/plugins/session-management).

**Moderation** <br />Log moderations with `/moderate`, and browse, view and edit them with `/moderations`, `/moderation-info` and `/edit-moderation`. See [Moderation](/pages/plugins/moderation).

**Shifts and tasks** <br />Clock in with `/shift`, and press **Resolve Task** or use `/task resolve` to finish your task and get the next one. See [Shifts](/pages/plugins/shifts) and [Tasks](/pages/plugins/tasks).

**Verification** <br />Link Roblox accounts with `/verify` and the verification panel, and keep roles up to date with `/update` and `/verification`. See [Verification](/pages/plugins/verification).

**Reports and appeals on the website** <br />Players report rule breaks and appeal moderations on the Elbify website, and your staff review them in Discord and on the dashboard.

## For Everyone

**Ten languages** <br />English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian and Turkish.

**Themes and accessibility** <br />Light, Elbify and Dark themes, plus Reduce Motion, High Contrast, Larger Text, Underline Links and a Dyslexia-Friendly Font in **Settings**.

**Roblox verification** <br />A verify page for each server, and one place to see and update every server you're verified in.

**Notifications, search and status** <br />Platform news in your profile menu, search across the website and these docs (press `/`), and a live status page.

## Elbify Plus

Everything in Elbify is free. **Elbify Plus** raises limits for servers with bigger staff teams, and belongs to a server rather than an account.

| Limit | Free | Plus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task types | 5 | 25 |
| Shift types | 3 | 25 |
| Permission roles | 10 | 50 |
| Moderation types | 5 | 25 |
| Custom forms | 1 | 5 |
| Roles per form decision | 1 | 5 |
| Data imports | 3 | 10 |
| Your own questions on reports and appeals | No | Yes |

If Plus ends, nothing is deleted. Your server goes back to the Free limits and everything you made keeps working.
