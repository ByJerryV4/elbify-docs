## In Discord

The Elbify bot has been updated alongside the website.

**Session transfers and polls** <br />Hand a session to another host or add co-hosts with `/session transfer`, and ask who would join with `/session poll`. See [Session Management](/pages/plugins/session-management).

**Moderations in Discord** <br />Browse, view and edit moderations with `/moderations`, `/moderation-info` and `/edit-moderation`. See [Moderation](/pages/plugins/moderation).

**Resolvable tasks** <br />Press **Resolve Task** or use `/task resolve` to finish your task and get the next one. See [Tasks](/pages/plugins/tasks).

**Reworked verification** <br />`/verify`, `/update` and `/verification`, with a clearer verification panel. See [Verification](/pages/plugins/verification).

**Reports and appeals move to the website** <br />`/report` and `/appeal` have been removed. Players submit reports and appeals on the Elbify website instead.

## Elbify Plus

Elbify Plus is now **per server**. Everything in Elbify stays free; Plus raises limits for servers with bigger staff teams.

| Limit | Free | Plus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task types | 5 | 25 |
| Shift types | 3 | 25 |
| Permission roles | 10 | 50 |
| Moderation types | 5 | 25 |
| Custom forms | 1 | 5 |
| Roles per form decision | 1 | 5 |
| Data imports | 3 | 10 |
| Your own questions on reports and appeals | No | Yes |

If Plus ends, nothing is deleted. Your server goes back to the Free limits and everything you made keeps working.
